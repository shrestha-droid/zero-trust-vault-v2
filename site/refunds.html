What happens after a refund

  • Pro ends. Your vaults keep working: nothing you've sealed is ever locked or deleted because a plan ended.

Renewals

Yearly plans renew automatically. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. If you were charged for a renewal you didn't want, contact us within 14 days of that charge and we'll refund it.

Payments and taxes

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, who also handles sales tax and VAT. Questions about a charge on your statement can go to them or to us.
