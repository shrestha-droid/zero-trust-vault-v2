How it works

Three steps. No one to trust.

Everything happens in your browser. The key is created on your device, used once, then destroyed. What's left is ciphertext nobody can read, and pieces of a key nobody can use alone.

01

Seal

Type a secret or drop any file up to 100 MB. It's padded and encrypted with AES-256-GCM in an isolated worker before anything is saved.

02

Split

Choose who can open it: any K of N shards, a passphrase, both together, or your passkey. Print a recovery kit with a QR code per person.

03

Recover

Bring the vault and enough shards. Typos are caught, forged shards are named and set aside, and the vault opens. Even with no internet, using the offline app.

Security

Verify, don't trust.

We built this so that we are never the weak point. Here's what that means in practice.

Keys never touch the page

All key handling runs in a dedicated Web Worker. Data keys and Shamir secrets never exist in the page's memory.

Independent key slots

Like disk encryption: shards, passphrase, both together, or passkey. Each slot is cryptographically bound to its vault.

Forged-shard detection

If a shard holder hands over a tampered shard, the app identifies it and still opens the vault from the honest ones.

Locked-down browser

A strict Content Security Policy and Trusted Types: no third-party scripts, no CDNs, no trackers, ever.

Post-quantum by design

No public-key cryptography in the vault format. AES-256 and Shamir leave a quantum computer nothing better than brute force.

Outlives us

The whole app is one HTML file that runs offline. Each release publishes it with a SHA-256 checksum. If we vanish, your vaults still open.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Can you see my secrets?

No. Encryption happens on your device before anything is stored, and the keys never leave it. Our servers only ever hold ciphertext, plus (if you use Legacy) your trustees' contact details, your message to them, and at most one shard, which on its own reveals nothing.

What if Zero-Trust Vault shuts down?

Your vaults still open. Download the offline app (one HTML file) and keep it next to your shards; it needs no server and no internet. The file format is documented, so anyone can audit or re-implement it.

What happens to Legacy if my payment fails after I'm gone?

It still fires. Pro is needed to set up or change a plan, but a plan that's already armed is always delivered. A deceased person's card eventually fails, and Legacy is designed around that.

What if I lose a shard, or someone forges one?

With "3 of 5", you can lose two shards safely. If someone hands over a tampered shard, the app detects it, tells you which one, and opens the vault from the rest. You can also re-key a vault any time to issue fresh shards.

Has it been audited?

The Shamir secret sharing library we use has been independently audited. The app itself has not yet been third-party audited. We'll publish that when it happens. Until then, the threat model and file format are documented in full.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Google, Apple or email only if you want vaults synced across devices or Legacy. You can also keep vaults in your own Google Drive.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app. It works offline, scans shard QR codes with the camera, and supports Face ID and fingerprint passkeys.
