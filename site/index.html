The method

Three articles. No one to trust.

Everything happens in your browser. The key is created on your device, used once, then destroyed. What remains is a locked file nobody can read, and coupons nobody can use alone.

I

Seal

Type a secret or drop any file up to 100 MB. It's padded and encrypted with AES-256-GCM in an isolated worker before anything is saved.

II

Split

The key becomes coupons: hand one each to people you trust. Any three of five open it; fewer reveal nothing at all. Print them as a recovery kit.

III

Recover

Bring the vault and enough coupons. Typos are caught, forged coupons are named and set aside, and it opens, even offline.

Specifications

Verify, don't trust.

We built this so that we are never the weak point. Every claim below is testable, and the file format is published in full.

Encryption
AES-256-GCM, authenticated. The header is bound to the ciphertext, so altering any byte fails.
Key splitting
Shamir secret sharing over GF(2⁸), via an independently audited library. Forged coupons are identified.
Passphrases
Argon2id, 64 MiB memory-hard, three passes.
Hardware keys
WebAuthn passkeys with the PRF extension: Touch ID, Windows Hello, YubiKey.
Isolation
Every key lives in a dedicated Web Worker, out of reach of the page and its extensions.
Hardening
Strict Content Security Policy and Trusted Types. No third-party scripts, no CDNs, no trackers.
Quantum
No public-key cryptography in the format. A quantum computer gets nothing better than brute force.
Longevity
One HTML file that runs offline, published with a SHA-256 checksum. If we vanish, your vaults still open.

Questions & answers

Questions worth asking.

The file format and threat model are published in full. Nothing here is a black box.

Q.Can you see my secrets?

A.No. Encryption happens on your device before anything is stored, and the keys never leave it. Our servers only ever hold locked files, plus, if you use Legacy, your trustees' contacts, your message to them, and at most one coupon, which on its own reveals nothing.

Q.What if Zero-Trust Vault shuts down?

A.Your vaults still open. Keep the offline app (one HTML file) next to your coupons: it needs no server and no internet. The file format is documented, so anyone can audit or re-implement it.

Q.What happens to Legacy if my payment fails after I'm gone?

A.It still fires. Pro is needed to set up or change a plan, but an armed plan is always delivered. A deceased person's card eventually fails, and Legacy is designed around that.

Q.What if a coupon is lost, or forged?

A.With "3 of 5" you can lose two safely. If someone hands over a tampered coupon, the app tells you which one and opens the vault from the rest. You can re-key at any time to issue fresh coupons.

Q.Has it been audited?

A.The secret-sharing library has been independently audited. The app itself hasn't been third-party audited yet. We'll publish that when it happens.

Q.Do I need an account?

A.No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Google, Apple or email only for vaults synced across devices or for Legacy. You can also keep vaults in your own Google Drive.

Q.Does it work on my phone?

A.Yes. Open it in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It works offline, scans coupons with the camera, and supports Face ID and fingerprint passkeys.
