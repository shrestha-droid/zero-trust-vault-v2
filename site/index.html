The method

Three moves. No one to trust.

Everything happens in your browser. The key is created on your device, used once, then destroyed. What remains is a locked file nobody can read, and pieces of a key nobody can use alone.

I

Seal

Type a secret or drop any file up to 100 MB. It's padded and encrypted with AES-256-GCM in an isolated worker before anything is saved.

II

Split

Choose who can open it: any three of five people, a passphrase, both together, or your passkey. Print a recovery kit with a QR code for each person.

III

Recover

Bring the vault and enough keys. Typos are caught, forged keys are named and set aside, and it opens, even offline.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

The file format and threat model are published in full. Nothing here is a black box.

Can you see my secrets?

No. Encryption happens on your device before anything is stored, and the keys never leave it. Our servers only ever hold locked files, plus, if you use Legacy, your trustees' contacts, your message to them, and at most one shard, which on its own reveals nothing.

What if Zero-Trust Vault shuts down?

Your vaults still open. Keep the offline app (one HTML file) next to your keys: it needs no server and no internet. The file format is documented, so anyone can audit or re-implement it.

What happens to Legacy if my payment fails after I'm gone?

It still fires. Pro is needed to set up or change a plan, but an armed plan is always delivered. A deceased person's card eventually fails, and Legacy is designed around that.

What if I lose a key, or someone forges one?

With "3 of 5" you can lose two safely. If someone hands over a tampered key, the app tells you which one and opens the vault from the rest. You can re-key at any time to issue fresh keys.

Has it been audited?

The secret-sharing library we use has been independently audited. The app itself hasn't been third-party audited yet. We'll publish that when it happens.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Google, Apple or email only for vaults synced across devices or for Legacy. You can also keep vaults in your own Google Drive.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It works offline, scans keys with the camera, and supports Face ID and fingerprint passkeys.
